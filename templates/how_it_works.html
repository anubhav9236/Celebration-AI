Methodology & Technology

Designed for Real Celebrations

CelebrationAI combines human-centered event design practices with Google Gemini generative AI to eliminate planning overwhelm while maintaining full budget control.

1

Context-Aware Event Modeling

Every event begins with your real constraints: date, headcount, location, budget ceiling, cultural traditions, dietary boundaries (e.g. Pure Vegetarian, Jain, Vegan, Mixed), and needed services.

Rather than generic advice, CelebrationAI adjusts milestones based on lead time (whether you have 9 months for a grand wedding or 3 weeks for an intimate birthday).

2

Generative AI & Mathematical Verification

When you generate a plan, Google Gemini (via the official google-genai SDK) synthesizes tailored decoration themes, color swatches, culinary tasting menus, and actionable tasks into structured JSON.

Mathematical Integrity Guarantee: Our backend verifies the AI's arithmetic. Total planned allocations are strictly capped at your total budget, and a dedicated 5–10% contingency buffer is automatically reserved for unforeseen incidentals.
3

The 7-in-1 Event Planner Workspace

Your plan is automatically converted into an interactive workspace with seven specialized coordination tools:

  • Overview: Live countdown ticker, progress bars, and high-priority alerts.
  • Budget: Interactive planned vs. actual expense tracker with Chart.js visualizations.
  • Timeline: Chronological milestone tasks with overdue task alerts.
  • Vendors: Booking workflows, contact records, and payment tracking.
  • Guest List: RSVP tracker, dietary requirements, and confirmed attendee counts.
  • Ideas: Curated creative concepts bookmarkable to your favorites.
  • Checklist: Shopping and day-of preparation checklists with cost tracking.
4

Conversational In-Dashboard Assistant

Need to cut ₹5,000, adapt to rain, or draft a vegetarian menu? The slide-out assistant has direct access to your event's current numbers and presents structured action proposals that you can review and apply with one click.
